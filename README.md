## Overview

This research note presents a WRRA dual-component description of observed gravity:

\[
g_{\mathrm{total}} = g_M + g_T,
\]

where \(g_M\) is the direct gravitational component generated by baryonic matter and \(g_T\) is the gravitational phenotype of stress stored in spatial twist and deformation. The decomposition is an effective weak-field bookkeeping structure, not an assertion that two independent fundamental forces must exist.

The model preserves the empirical success of the MOND low-acceleration relation while reinterpreting it as a nonlinear spatial response. Established constants and observed cosmological quantities are used to calibrate a toy model of our universe. Once calibrated, the closure relation and response function must be frozen and tested against other phenomena without galaxy-by-galaxy retuning.

## Main Quantitative Results

- Present-universe closure scale:

  \[
  a_T=cH_0\sqrt{f_T/8}=1.1918\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.
  \]

- SPARC deep-low-acceleration diagnostic:

  \[
  a_{T,\mathrm{SPARC}}=1.2007\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}},
  \]

  giving a difference of approximately 0.75% from the WRRA value.

- Present-universe equivalent-source ratio:

  \[
  \frac{g_T}{g_M}=\frac{\Omega_T}{\Omega_b}=5.374.
  \]

  Within these two attractive components, the corresponding shares are approximately 84.3% for twist stress and 15.7% for direct baryonic gravity.

- Transition radius for an isolated mass:

  \[
  r_T=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{a_T}}.
  \]

- Surface-density thresholds:

  \[
  \Sigma_*^{\mathrm{sph}}\approx272\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{pc^{-2}},
  \qquad
  \Sigma_*^{\mathrm{disk}}\approx136\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{pc^{-2}}.
  \]

## Research Status

The numerical agreement is a meaningful reality-consistency result: the WRRA structure connects known cosmological inputs to the observed galactic acceleration scale. The factor \(1/8\) in the closure relation is a calibrated cross-scale connection and is stated explicitly as such.

The physical identification of the additional component with spatial twist stress remains a causal hypothesis. Its explanatory status will be strengthened or rejected by cross-scale tests using one frozen response function. WRRA's distinctive contribution lies in the decomposition, transformation, and connection among mass gravity, spatial stress, MOND phenomenology, disk geometry, and cosmological component ratios.
